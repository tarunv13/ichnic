# Funding Ichnic

Ichnic is free to use, open to reuse and run by one maintainer. It costs little to run (GitHub
Actions and Pages are free for public projects) but a lot of skilled time to keep accurate: every
audit, new language, new source and new species group is work. Funding decides how fast the gaps in
the [research agenda](RESEARCH_AGENDA.md) close.

## What funding buys

| Tier | What it pays for | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keep it accurate** | a quarterly blind accuracy audit, review of ambiguous words and code words, source maintenance | precision measured and published every quarter ([ACCURACY.md](ACCURACY.md)) |
| **Widen the evidence** | new languages (Chinese, Spanish-language Africa, Swahili), court-outcome sources, port-level trade data | fewer blind spots; link 4 of the [risk pathways](https://tarunv13.github.io/wildtrace/pathways.html) (who profits) begins to close |
| **Partner deployments** | a version of the Atlas and workbench for one enforcement agency, NGO or newsroom, with their own private data kept on their machines | evidence that reaches a decision |
| **Research** | an open event-extraction benchmark, stance detection for listings, image evidence at scale (agenda A1, A2, A6) | peer-reviewed methods and datasets anyone can use |

## Routes being pursued

- **UK Illegal Wildlife Trade Challenge Fund** (Defra, Biodiversity Challenge Funds): funds projects that strengthen
  law enforcement, legal frameworks, demand reduction and livelihoods. Ichnic fits as evidence infrastructure inside a
  project led with an implementing partner. [iwt.challengefund.org.uk](https://iwt.challengefund.org.uk/)
- **WILDLABS Awards** (with Arm): annual conservation-technology grants of $10,000 and $50,000.
  [wildlabs.net](https://wildlabs.net/)
- **Open-source infrastructure funds** in Europe (NLnet's NGI programmes are moving to the EU Open Internet Stack): open
  data and open science tooling. [nlnet.nl](https://nlnet.nl/)
- **Research funding with an academic partner**: doctoral and postdoctoral routes in conservation science and computational
  social science, where Ichnic is the open instrument of the research.

## How to support it now

- **Use it and cite it**: citations are what funders read. Cite the version you used (see the README).
- **Partner**: agencies, NGOs, newsrooms and universities who want Ichnic's evidence in their work, open an issue titled
  "Partnership" or write through the maintainer's site, [tarunverma.com](https://tarunverma.com).
- **Contribute**: pick a question from the [research agenda](RESEARCH_AGENDA.md).

Funding never buys influence over what the data shows. Methods, audits and limits stay public, and no funder
receives private data about people.
