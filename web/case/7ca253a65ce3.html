Seizure · Parrots & songbirds, Elephant (ivory), Leopard, Pangolin, Tiger, Tokay gecko, Turtles & tortoises · 12.7 tonnes · Gajapati

27 July 2024 · Gajapati · official: reported by at least one government, customs, police or judicial source.

A seizure involving parrots & songbirds, elephant (ivory), leopard, pangolin, tiger, tokay gecko, turtles & tortoises was first reported on 27 July 2024, in Gajapati, Odisha, IN.

The reports give the quantity as 11.9 tonnes, 12.7 tonnes, 164 scales, 2 skins, 2200 pangolins, 25.6 tonnes, 8.8 tonnes, 830 kg.

6 people were reported arrested. WildTrace records the number only, never who they are, and an arrest is not a conviction.

Crime Branch, Customs, DRI, Forest Department, Police, STF, WCCB are named as acting in the reports.

The case is drawn from 197 report(s), reported by at least one government, customs, police or judicial source.
