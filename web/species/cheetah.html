Cheetah in the illegal wildlife trade

Ichnic has recorded 3 cases involving cheetah from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-10-20, in 0 countries. 0 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I. Taxa covered: Acinonyx jubatus. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

    Seized at US ports

    20 import records of cheetah were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: skin (substantially whole, including tinga frames) (8), skin piece (raw or tanned including scraps) (4), medicinal part or product (4), trophy (all the parts of one animal) (2), skull (1). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
