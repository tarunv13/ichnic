Monitor lizard in the illegal wildlife trade

Ichnic has recorded 45 cases involving monitor lizard from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-10-23, in 8 countries. 1 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I. Taxa covered: Varanus bengalensis, Varanus flavescens, Varanus salvator. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

360 import records of monitor lizard were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: leather product (small manufactured including belt, wallet, watchband) (182), shoe (including boots) (53), leather product (large manufactured including briefcase, suitcase, furniture) (35), trim (shoe, garment, or decorative) (21), live specimens (live animals or plants) (15). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
