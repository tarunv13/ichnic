Hornbills in the illegal wildlife trade

Ichnic has recorded 20 cases involving hornbills from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-10-19, in 5 countries. 2 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I/II. Taxa covered: Rhinoplax vigil, Bucerotidae. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

25 import records of hornbills were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: skull (14), unspecified (4), skeleton (substantially whole) (2), feathers (1), medicinal part or product (1). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
