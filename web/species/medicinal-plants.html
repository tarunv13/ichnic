Medicinal & aromatic plants in the illegal wildlife trade

Ichnic has recorded 2 cases involving medicinal & aromatic plants from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-10-29, in 1 countries. 2 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I/II. Taxa covered: Saussurea costus, Nardostachys jatamansi, Panax quinquefolius, Prunus africana, Taxus wallichiana, Aquilaria, Rauvolfia serpentina. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

134 import records of medicinal & aromatic plants were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: medicinal part or product (122), root (dead) (8), powder (2), unspecified (1), live specimens (live animals or plants) (1). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
