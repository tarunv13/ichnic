Tiger in the illegal wildlife trade

Ichnic has recorded 91 cases involving tiger from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-10-30, in 10 countries. 12 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I. Taxa covered: Panthera tigris. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

632 import records of tiger were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: medicinal part or product (490), claw (including talon) (54), teeth (excluding tusk) (39), unspecified (7), jewelry (other than ivory jewelry) (6). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
