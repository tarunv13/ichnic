Cacti & succulents in the illegal wildlife trade

Ichnic has recorded 6 cases involving cacti & succulents from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-10-20, in 3 countries. 2 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I/II. Taxa covered: Cactaceae, Conophytum spp., Lithops spp., Ariocarpus spp., Euphorbia spp., Dudleya spp.. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

10 import records of cacti & succulents were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: live specimens (live animals or plants) (4), wood product (including furniture, rainsticks) (2), stems (plant stems) (1), cuttings (plant cuttings or divisions) (1), unspecified (1). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
