Sea fan & corals in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 7 cases involving sea fan & corals from public reports between 2024-01-19 and 2026-10-17, in 2 countries. 0 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix II. Taxa covered: Gorgonacea, Scleractinia. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

3,718 import records of sea fan & corals were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: live specimens (live animals or plants) (1,426), coral (raw or unworked, excluding live or coral rock) (1,187), coral products (406), jewelry (other than ivory jewelry) (311), live rock (e.g., coral rock) (265). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
