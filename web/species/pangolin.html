Pangolin in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 217 cases involving pangolin from public reports between 2024-01-19 and 2026-10-13, in 17 countries. 18 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I. Taxa covered: Manis crassicaudata, Manis javanica, Manis pentadactyla. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

60 import records of pangolin were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: medicinal part or product (43), scale (turtle, other reptile, fish, pangolin) (7), skin (substantially whole, including tinga frames) (3), dead animal (whole animal) (1), shoe (including boots) (1). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
