Jaguar in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 15 cases involving jaguar from public reports between 2024-01-19 and 2026-10-19, in 5 countries. 1 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I. Taxa covered: Panthera onca. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

66 import records of jaguar were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: skin (substantially whole, including tinga frames) (25), teeth (excluding tusk) (15), garment (excluding shoe or trim) (5), skull (4), leather product (small manufactured including belt, wallet, watchband) (4). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
