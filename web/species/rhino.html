Rhinoceros in the illegal wildlife trade

Ichnic has recorded 82 cases involving rhinoceros from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-10-29, in 12 countries. 16 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I. Taxa covered: Rhinoceros unicornis, Rhinoceros sondaicus. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

37 import records of rhinoceros were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: trophy (all the parts of one animal) (9), foot (6), specimen (scientific or museum) (4), horn carving (including horn or antler products) (3), skin piece (raw or tanned including scraps) (2). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
