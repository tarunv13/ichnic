Agarwood (oud) in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 19 cases involving agarwood (oud) from public reports between 2024-01-19 and 2026-10-16, in 7 countries. 2 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix II. Taxa covered: Aquilaria spp., Gyrinops spp.. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

279 import records of agarwood (oud) were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: wood product (including furniture, rainsticks) (111), chips (timber) (89), oil (44), medicinal part or product (25), derivative (except those included elsewhere) (4). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
