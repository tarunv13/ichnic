Turtles & tortoises in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 234 cases involving turtles & tortoises from public reports between 2024-01-19 and 2026-10-11, in 24 countries. 28 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I/II. Taxa covered: Geochelone elegans, Nilssonia gangetica, Lissemys punctata, Batagur spp.. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

1,094 import records of turtles & tortoises were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: live specimens (live animals or plants) (294), carapaces (raw or unworked) (174), dead animal (whole animal) (134), medicinal part or product (111), shell (mollusc, raw or unworked) (93). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
