Pythons & reptile skins in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 21 cases involving pythons & reptile skins from public reports between 2024-01-19 and 2026-10-11, in 8 countries. 0 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I/II. Taxa covered: Python spp., Crocodylidae, Varanus salvator. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

5,285 import records of pythons & reptile skins were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: leather product (small manufactured including belt, wallet, watchband) (2,633), shoe (including boots) (807), leather product (large manufactured including briefcase, suitcase, furniture) (563), skin (substantially whole, including tinga frames) (233), meat (175). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
