Sharks, rays & sea cucumbers in the illegal wildlife trade

Ichnic has recorded 125 cases involving sharks, rays & sea cucumbers from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-11-12, in 15 countries. 20 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix II. Taxa covered: Selachimorpha, Batoidea, Holothuroidea, Hippocampus spp.. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

1,644 import records of sharks, rays & sea cucumbers were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: dead animal (whole animal) (584), medicinal part or product (338), powder (256), leather product (small manufactured including belt, wallet, watchband) (127), shoe (including boots) (69). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
