Eels in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 25 cases involving eels from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-10-19, in 3 countries. 3 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix II. Taxa covered: Anguilla anguilla, Anguilla spp.. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

45 import records of eels were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: shoe (including boots) (20), leather product (small manufactured including belt, wallet, watchband) (14), live specimens (live animals or plants) (3), skin piece (raw or tanned including scraps) (3), garment (excluding shoe or trim) (2). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
