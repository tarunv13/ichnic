Orchids in the illegal wildlife trade

Ichnic has recorded 2 cases involving orchids from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-10-27, in 1 countries. 2 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I/II. Taxa covered: Orchidaceae, Cymbidium spp., Dendrobium spp., Paphiopedilum spp., Dactylorhiza spp., Orchis spp.. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

108 import records of orchids were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: medicinal part or product (91), live specimens (live animals or plants) (3), dried plants (3), cuttings (plant cuttings or divisions) (3), powder (2). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
