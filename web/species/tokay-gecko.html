Tokay gecko in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 12 cases involving tokay gecko from public reports between 2024-01-19 and 2026-10-13, in 3 countries. 3 of them rest on an official or validated source.

Seized at US ports

75 import records of tokay gecko were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: dead animal (whole animal) (33), medicinal part or product (25), skeleton (substantially whole) (4), meat (4), skull (3). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
