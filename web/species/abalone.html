Abalone in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 63 cases involving abalone from public reports between 2024-01-19 and 2026-10-11, in 5 countries. 0 of them rest on an official or validated source.

Taxa covered: Haliotis midae, Haliotis spp.. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

229 import records of abalone were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: jewelry (other than ivory jewelry) (104), shell product (mollusc or turtle) (81), shell (mollusc, raw or unworked) (30), trim (shoe, garment, or decorative) (9), unspecified (1). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
