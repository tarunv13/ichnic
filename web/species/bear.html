Bears in the illegal wildlife trade

Ichnic has recorded 16 cases involving bears from public reports between 2024-01-04 and 2026-10-30, in 6 countries. 1 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I. Taxa covered: Melursus ursinus, Helarctos malayanus, Ursus thibetanus. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

943 import records of bears were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: medicinal part or product (311), claw (including talon) (89), skull (73), trophy (all the parts of one animal) (71), skin (substantially whole, including tinga frames) (62). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
