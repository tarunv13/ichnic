Owls in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 7 cases involving owls from public reports between 2024-01-19 and 2026-10-14, in 1 countries. 0 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix II. Taxa covered: Strigiformes. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

259 import records of owls were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: feathers (111), dead animal (whole animal) (47), trophy (all the parts of one animal) (35), foot (23), claw (including talon) (14). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
