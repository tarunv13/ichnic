Golden jackal in the illegal wildlife trade

No news report of a golden jackal seizure has reached Ichnic yet. The trade is still recorded: countries report seized and confiscated shipments to CITES.

CITES appendix III. Taxa covered: Canis aureus.

Where seized golden jackal came from, and where it was seized

9 shipments of golden jackal seized or confiscated were reported to CITES since 2015 (CITES Trade Database, source code I). Counts are shipment records, not quantities, and some countries report far more completely than others.

Seized at US ports

13 import records of golden jackal were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: trophy (all the parts of one animal) (9), dead animal (whole animal) (2), skin (substantially whole, including tinga frames) (2). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
