Are plants trafficked as much as animals?

Plants are 15% of seized shipments reported to CITES but only 10% of news cases: timber, orchids and medicinal plants are trafficked far more than they are reported.

15% of CITES seizures are plants

  • Plants in CITES seizures (%)15
  • Plants in news cases (%)10

What you should know

CITES lists many more plant species than news ever names; plant blindness cuts both ways.

Sources and processing

  • CITES Trade Database seized shipments (source I), reported by governments to UNEP-WCMC
  • Public reports (Ichnic) 348 official, 407 corroborated, 1,697 single report

Figures are computed from the published Ichnic data on every daily build; counts are counts of public reports collected, not the true incidence of the crime.
