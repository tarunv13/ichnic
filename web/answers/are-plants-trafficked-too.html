Are plants trafficked as much as animals?

Plants are 15% of seized shipments reported to CITES but only 11% of news cases: timber, orchids and medicinal plants are trafficked far more than they are reported.

15% of CITES seizures are plants

  • Plants in CITES seizures (%)15
  • Plants in news cases (%)11

Who answers

  • CITES Trade Database seized shipments (source I), reported by governments to UNEP-WCMC
  • Public reports (WildTrace) 233 official, 365 corroborated, 1,550 single report

What the number cannot tell you

CITES lists many more plant species than news ever names; plant blindness cuts both ways.
