Which wildlife is seized most often?

Parrots & songbirds lead the public record with 343 cases, then turtles & tortoises (272) and elephant (ivory) (231).

343 cases involving parrots & songbirds

  • Parrots & songbirds343
  • Turtles & tortoises272
  • Elephant (ivory)231
  • Pangolin229
  • Rosewood & timber95
  • Sharks, rays & sea cucumbers94

What you should know

Cases count reporting, not trafficking volume: well-covered species and countries look bigger.

Sources and processing

  • Public reports (Ichnic) 324 official, 374 corroborated, 1,593 single report

Figures are computed from the published Ichnic data on every daily build; counts are counts of public reports collected, not the true incidence of the crime.
