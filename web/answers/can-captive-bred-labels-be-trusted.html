Can "captive-bred" labels be trusted?

Not always: declaring wild-caught animals as bred is a documented laundering route. Palau's commercial sea fan & corals exports moved from 0% to 97% captive-bred in a few years.

What you should know

A rise is a question to ask, never a finding: genuine breeding and coral farming produce it too.
