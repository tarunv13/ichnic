Does wildlife trade overlap with disease outbreaks?

Viruses confirmed by sequencing or isolation are recorded in 23 traded groups, and WHO published 2,120 reports on diseases with an animal reservoir; the two share a map, not a cause.

2,120 WHO outbreak reports on animal-borne disease

  • Primates1,034

  • Turtles & tortoises138
  • Bears74
  • Pythons & reptile skins40

What you should know

Virus records follow where scientists sampled; an outbreak near a seizure does not mean the trade caused it.

Sources and processing

  • VIRION (Carlson et al. 2022) virus-host records
  • WHO Disease Outbreak News outbreak reports

Figures are computed from the published Ichnic data on every daily build; counts are counts of public reports collected, not the true incidence of the crime.
