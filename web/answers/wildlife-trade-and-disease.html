Does wildlife trade overlap with disease outbreaks?

Viruses confirmed by sequencing or isolation are recorded in 23 traded groups, and WHO published 2,119 reports on diseases with an animal reservoir; the two share a map, not a cause.

What the number cannot tell you

Virus records follow where scientists sampled; an outbreak near a seizure does not mean the trade caused it.
