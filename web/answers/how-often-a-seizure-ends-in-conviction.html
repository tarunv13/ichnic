How often does a seizure end in a conviction?

In the public record, for every conviction there are about 10 seizures: 1,351 seizures, 585 arrest reports and 130 convictions.

130 convictions in the record

  • Seizures1,351
  • Arrests585
  • Rescues197
  • Convictions130

What you should know

Courts are reported far less than seizures, so part of the drop is a reporting gap, not only attrition.

Sources and processing

  • Public reports (Ichnic) 324 official, 370 corroborated, 1,570 single report

Figures are computed from the published Ichnic data on every daily build; counts are counts of public reports collected, not the true incidence of the crime.
