What is seized at US ports, and where does it come from?

US wildlife inspectors seized 19,346 import records of the groups Ichnic follows; most came from China, Indonesia, Mexico.

19,346 import records seized by US inspectors

  • China2,018
  • Indonesia1,863
  • Mexico1,801
  • United States1,439
  • Vietnam1,202
  • Thailand921

What you should know

It measures where US inspection is strong as much as where trade flows.

Sources and processing

  • US Fish and Wildlife Service LEMIS cleaned by Marshall et al. 2025 and Eskew et al. 2020

Figures are computed from the published Ichnic data on every daily build; counts are counts of public reports collected, not the true incidence of the crime.
