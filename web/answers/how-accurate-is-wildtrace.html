How accurate is WildTrace?

In a blind audit of 200 randomly sampled cases, 193 were genuine wildlife-trade enforcement events (96.5%; 95% interval 93.0% to 98.3%), and species were right in 98.4% of them.

96.5% of audited cases are real events (2026-09-29)

What you should know

Precision says how many published cases are real, not how many real events are missed. Against an independent list of 576 hand-checked events, WildTrace finds about one in five once chance matches are removed (docs/ACCURACY.md); coverage follows the sources searched.

Sources and processing

  • WildTrace accuracy audit blind random sample of published cases, each judged against its source headlines by a reviewer
  • Public reports (WildTrace) 292 official, 372 corroborated, 1,559 single report

Figures are computed from the published WildTrace data on every daily build; counts are counts of public reports collected, not the true incidence of the crime.
