How accurate is Ichnic?

In a blind audit of 200 randomly sampled cases, 193 were genuine wildlife-trade enforcement events (96.5%; 95% interval 93.0% to 98.3%), and species were right in 98.4% of them.

96.5% of audited cases are real events (2026-09-29)

What you should know

Precision says how many published cases are real, not how many real events are missed. Recall, the share of real events Ichnic finds, is about 20% to 25%: that is how many of 576 hand-checked events on an independent list it matched, after removing matches that happen by chance. Coverage follows the sources searched.

Sources and processing

  • Ichnic accuracy audit blind random sample of published cases, each judged against its source headlines by a reviewer
  • Public reports (Ichnic) 348 official, 407 corroborated, 1,697 single report

Figures are computed from the published Ichnic data on every daily build; counts are counts of public reports collected, not the true incidence of the crime.
