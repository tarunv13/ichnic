Where does seized wildlife come from, and where is it caught?

Leaving the United States aside, the busiest seized route is India to China (333 shipments); US-bound routes are 83% of all 24,786 seized shipments reported to CITES.

24,786 seized shipments reported to CITES since 2015

  • India → China333
  • India → Hong Kong97
  • Vietnam → Canada66
  • Sri Lanka → United Arab Emirates58
  • Vietnam → South Korea57
  • Guatemala → Germany44

What you should know

Countries report seizures unevenly; the United States reports far more completely than most.

Sources and processing

  • CITES Trade Database seized shipments (source I), reported by governments to UNEP-WCMC

Figures are computed from the published Ichnic data on every daily build; counts are counts of public reports collected, not the true incidence of the crime.
