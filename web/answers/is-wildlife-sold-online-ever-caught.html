Is wildlife sold online ever caught?

Rarely, in the public record: Bears in Thailand drew 1,303 adverts and 0 Ichnic cases in the same period.

30,934 adverts recorded by ECO-SOLVE, 2024-04 to 2026-09

  • Bears, Thailand1,303
  • Parrots & songbirds, Thailand1,046
  • Elephant (ivory), Thailand2,917
  • Parrots & songbirds, Indonesia859
  • Parrots & songbirds, Colombia1,154
  • Tiger, Thailand2,232

What you should know

Adverts and cases are recorded with different effort; the ratio is not a detection rate.

Sources and processing

  • ECO-SOLVE (Global Initiative Against Transnational Organized Crime) adverts recorded by regional hubs
  • Public reports (Ichnic) 324 official, 370 corroborated, 1,570 single report

Figures are computed from the published Ichnic data on every daily build; counts are counts of public reports collected, not the true incidence of the crime.
